## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- Soft-3D floating island with cottage, pond, tree, market stand, lantern and fenced 6×4 plot.
- Core loop: till, plant, water, sleep, harvest, sell.
- Five crops (carrot, tomato, strawberry, sunflower, pumpkin); tomatoes regrow after harvest.
- Seeded weather: rain waters the garden.
- Three visitors with requests that reward coins and unlock seeds.
- Bloom meter with level-ups that unlock crops and add butterflies.
- Five decorations for the market's Decor tab.
- Farmer with click-to-walk (A* pathfinding) and a cat companion.
- Day/dusk lighting, fireflies, particle bursts, synthesized sounds.
- Auto-save with versioned, validated saves; export/import; new game.
- Keyboard controls, mobile layout, reduced-motion support.
- Vitest unit/integration tests, Playwright e2e, Husky hooks, GitHub Actions CI/CD.
